CLEAR ANSWERS // FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

How does FETHO reconcile academic entrance exams (JEE/NEET) with holistic activities? +
Our dual-track pedagogy teaches every topic at foundational first-principles depth. A single concept is taught thoroughly once, fulfilling subjective board presentation requirements while solving advanced competitive numerical formats. Concurrently, yoga and breathwork are scheduled to optimize neurological recovery, directly preventing student burnout.
What is the advantage of the multi-pillar Growth Club model? +
Instead of coordinating 4-5 isolated centers, parents gain one harmonious developmental hub. Chess builds spatial reasoning for physics, public speaking trains argumentative poise for interviews, and physical conditioning builds stamina for demanding 3-hour national exam blocks.
What is the significance of the 12:1 student-to-mentor ratio? +
Unlike mega-batches of 100+ students where quiet doubts go unnoticed, our faculty review individual weekly test error patterns and conduct mandatory 1-on-1 corrective mentoring sessions every single Saturday.
What are the admissions criteria and scholarship options? +
Admissions are granted based on our proprietary diagnostic aptitude evaluation and student personal interview. Merit scholarships covering up to 100% of academic fees are awarded to exceptional national talents.
