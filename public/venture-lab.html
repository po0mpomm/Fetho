FETHO VENTURE LAB

Disciplined
Validation.

Most startups fail before they begin — not from lack of passion, but from lack of evidence. FETHO Venture Lab applies a rigorous six-stage validation protocol to separate viable ideas from wishful thinking. No shortcuts. No empty promises.

Submit Your Idea See The Process
VALIDATION PROTOCOL

Six Stages.
Zero Assumptions.

Each stage is a gate. Ideas that cannot pass on evidence do not advance. This is not an incubator — it is a disciplined scientific protocol for reducing uncertainty.

STAGE 01
💡
Idea Intake

Structured submission: founder background, problem hypothesis, proposed solution, and initial market intuition. No pitch decks — just clear thinking.

↳ INTAKE SCORECARD
STAGE 02
🔍 
Problem Validation

60+ customer discovery interviews, secondary research synthesis, and pain-severity ranking. The problem must be real before the solution matters.

↳ PROBLEM EVIDENCE REPORT
STAGE 03
⚖️ 
Feasibility Assessment

Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), competitive mapping, regulatory scan, and founding team capability analysis against the execution requirements.

↳ FEASIBILITY MATRIX
STAGE 04
📍 Š
Evidence-Based Decision

Expert panel review of all gathered evidence. A structured go/no-go/pivot decision is issued with documented reasoning — not gut feel, never politics.

↳ GO / NO-GO / PIVOT
STAGE 05
🚀
MVP Launch Planning

For validated ideas: 90-day MVP roadmap, hypothesis-to-feature mapping, success metrics definition, and early adopter acquisition strategy.

↳ 90-DAY MVP ROADMAP
STAGE 06
🌱
Growth Support

Post-launch monitoring, iteration coaching, mentor introductions, and ecosystem connections. We track outcomes — not just outputs.

↳ SUSTAINED ADVISORY
OUR BOUNDARY & COMMITMENT

Validation is science.
Not a promise of funding.

We are transparent about what FETHO Venture Lab is — and what it is not. Our protocol is a disciplined scientific process for reducing uncertainty. It does not guarantee commercial success, funding, or revenue. What it guarantees is rigor, honesty, and clarity — things the startup ecosystem desperately lacks.

✓
We do: Apply rigorous validation methodology, provide honest expert feedback, and give founders evidence-backed clarity on their idea's viability.
✕
We do not: Guarantee investment, promise revenue, manufacture optimism, or advance ideas on the strength of enthusiasm alone.
✓
Our commitment: Every founder receives a documented, evidence-based outcome — whether that is a validated path forward, a recommended pivot, or an honest assessment that the timing is not right.
